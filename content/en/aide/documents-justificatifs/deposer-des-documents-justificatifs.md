## Before you start

- This article is for the contributor. They have no account: everything happens from the link they receive by email, with no password.
- The **subscription model** must request supporting documents: the manager defined them at the model's **Contrat** (Contract) step. See [Define the supporting documents required](/en/aide/modeles-de-souscription/definir-les-documents-justificatifs-demandes).
- The manager must have sent the contract for signature. See [Send a contract for signature](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/envoyer-un-contrat-en-signature).
- Have your files ready in PDF, JPEG or PNG format (a photo taken with a phone is fine).

## Steps

### 1. Open the link received by email

In the invitation email, click the link: the **Signature du contrat** (Contract signature) page opens. If the manager left some information for the contributor to complete (company name, address, SIRET…), you are asked for it first; the supporting documents only appear afterwards. See [Sign a contract (contributor)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/signer-un-contrat).

Read the contract: the text is displayed at the top of the page, followed by any annexes.

### 2. Upload each document in the Justificatifs à fournir card

Below the contract, the **Justificatifs à fournir** (Supporting documents to provide) card shows one field per requested document, named as in the subscription model (for example « Pièce d'identité » (ID document)). All of them are required.

In each field, drag your file or click the area (« Glissez un fichier ici ou cliquez pour le choisir » (Drag a file here or click to choose one)). Only PDF, JPEG and PNG files are offered. The message « Envoi en cours… » (Upload in progress…) is displayed during the upload, then the file name appears in the field: click it to check that it is the right one (it opens in a new tab).

To change a file, simply choose another one in the same area (« …cliquez pour le remplacer » (…click to replace it)): it replaces the first one. There is no button to remove a document, since they are all required.

If the upload fails, a red message is displayed under the field (« Échec de l'envoi du fichier vers le stockage. » (Failed to send the file to storage.) for example): choose the file again.

### 3. Certify that you have read the contract and sign

Turn on the **Je certifie avoir lu le contrat** (I certify that I have read the contract) switch, then click **Signer** (Sign).

As long as a document is missing, the screen warns you: « Envoyez d'abord tous les justificatifs requis ci-dessus pour pouvoir signer. » (First send all the required supporting documents above in order to sign.) If you submit anyway, the message « Ce justificatif est requis. » (This supporting document is required.) appears under each empty field and the signature is not recorded. Your files are only sent to the manager at the moment of signing.

Once signed, the **En attente** (Pending) card replaces the form: « Vos pièces justificatives sont en cours de validation. Le signataire signera ensuite le contrat. » (Your supporting documents are being reviewed. The signer will then sign the contract.)

## What happens next?

- **Review by the manager**: the contract moves to the status **Documents à valider** (Documents to approve). The manager receives an email, and you receive an email telling you that your documents are being reviewed. The manager opens each file and accepts or rejects it. See [Approve or reject supporting documents](/en/aide/documents-justificatifs/valider-ou-rejeter-des-documents-justificatifs).
- **If everything is accepted**: the contract awaits the signer's signature. See [Countersign a contract (signer)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/contresigner-un-contrat). Since documents are requested, you do not receive the « contrat en cours de signature » (contract being signed) email: the one about the review takes its place.
- **If at least one document is rejected**: the contract moves to the status **Documents à mettre à jour** (Documents to update) and you receive an email listing the rejected documents, with the manager's reason, and the same link. On the page, the **Justificatifs** (Supporting documents) card shows each document with its status (**En attente** (Pending), **Accepté** (Accepted) or **Rejeté** (Rejected)) and the reason for the rejection in red. The **Justificatifs à fournir** card only asks again for the rejected documents (or those never uploaded): upload the new files, certify that you have read the contract and sign again. Documents already accepted or pending do not change, and the review cycle starts over.
- When the signer has signed in turn, the contract is signed. See [Sign a contract (contributor)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/signer-un-contrat).

## Good to know

- Documents can only be uploaded at the moment of signing the contract: there is no screen to add some afterwards.
- Cascade does not impose a maximum size. The PDF, JPEG and PNG formats are offered by the file picker, but Cascade does not check the content: the manager judges at review.
- One file per document. To send several pages, combine them into a single PDF.
- The link received by email expires 10 days after the subscription's effective date. After that, the page no longer opens: the manager must resend the invitation. See [Resend the invitation to sign](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/relancer-l-invitation-a-signer).
- A file uploaded but never sent with a signature (tab closed, page left) is deleted automatically, between 24 and 48 hours later. See [What Cascade does every day](/en/aide/general/ce-que-cascade-fait-chaque-jour). You then just need to upload it again.
- Documents are not requested if the manager added the contract without an electronic signature. See [Add a contract without a signature (already signed on paper)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/ajouter-un-contrat-sans-signature).
- Documents are only requested when signing the initial contract, not an amendment. See [Make an amendment](/en/aide/souscriptions/faire-un-avenant).

## Frequently asked questions

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="The button won't let me sign."}
Check that each field of the **Justificatifs à fournir** (Supporting documents to provide) card contains a file, and that the reading switch is turned on.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="My document was rejected, what should I do?"}
Open the link from the email you received again, upload only the rejected documents, taking the stated reason into account, then sign again.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="The link no longer opens."}
It expires 10 days after the subscription's effective date. Ask the manager to resend the invitation.
:::
::
