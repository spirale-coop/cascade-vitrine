## Before you start

- You need an account and you must be signed in. See [Sign in](/en/aide/acces-et-compte/se-connecter).
- To change your password, you must know your current password.

## Steps

### 1. Open your account

At the bottom of the left menu, click your name, then **Mon compte** (My account).

![The account menu, at the bottom left, with the Mon compte (My account) entry](/aide/modifier-son-compte/modifier-compte-01-menu.png)

### 2. Edit your personal details

In the **Informations personnelles** (Personal information) section, you can change your **Prénom** (First name), your **Nom** (Last name) and your **Email**. All three are required, and two accounts cannot have the same email.

![The Informations personnelles (Personal information) section](/aide/modifier-son-compte/modifier-compte-02-infos.png)

### 3. Change your password (optional)

In the **Changer de mot de passe** (Change password) section:

- **Mot de passe actuel** (Current password): to be entered only if you are changing your password.
- **Nouveau mot de passe** (New password): leave this field empty to keep your current password.
- **Confirmation du nouveau mot de passe** (New password confirmation): must be identical to the new password.

Errors appear as soon as you leave a field, for example « Le mot de passe est incorrect. » (The password is incorrect.) or « Le champ de confirmation ne correspond pas. » (The confirmation field does not match.), along with a « Corrigez les erreurs du formulaire avant de continuer. » (Fix the form errors before continuing.) notification.

![The errors displayed when the current password is wrong and the confirmation does not match](/aide/modifier-son-compte/modifier-compte-03-mot-de-passe.png)

### 4. Choose your theme

In the **Thème** (Theme) section, open the list and choose Cascade's appearance: **System** (your device's), **Light** or **Dark**. These three labels are displayed in English on screen. The change applies immediately, without waiting for you to save.

![The list of the three themes](/aide/modifier-son-compte/modifier-compte-04-theme.png)

![Cascade with the Dark theme](/aide/modifier-son-compte/modifier-compte-04b-sombre.png)

### 5. Save

Click **Enregistrer** (Save), at the bottom of the page.

![The Enregistrer (Save) button](/aide/modifier-son-compte/modifier-compte-05-enregistrer.png)

You stay on the "Mon compte" (My account) page, with the message **Profil modifié avec succès.** (Profile successfully updated.) The password fields are cleared.

![The confirmation message after saving](/aide/modifier-son-compte/modifier-compte-06-confirmation.png)

## What happens next?

- Your new details are taken into account right away: your name appears at the bottom of the menu, and your new password is used the next time you sign in.
- No confirmation email is sent, either to the old or to the new address.

## Good to know

- The **Nom** (Last name) is always saved in capitals, however you type it.
- The current password is only requested if you fill in the new password. If you don't touch the password section, you can save your details without entering it.
- In production, the new password must be at least 8 characters long and must not appear in a known password leak.
- This screen does not let you reset a forgotten password: you have to ask an administrator. It does not let you change your organizations or your rights either. See Create and manage users (linking users to organizations).
- Your account is personal: you can only edit your own.

## Frequently asked questions

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="I changed the theme but did not save it. Is it lost?"}
No: the theme applies as soon as you choose it. It is remembered by your browser, not by your account: on another device or another browser, you will get the System theme again.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="I changed my email. Which address do I sign in with?"}
With the new one, as soon as you save.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="My save is refused."}
Read the messages under the fields: email already used, incorrect current password, confirmation different from the new password, or an empty required field.
:::
::
