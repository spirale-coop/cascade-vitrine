## Before you start

- You need a Cascade account. Accounts are created by the Cascade team: see Create and manage users (linking users to organizations). You cannot sign up by yourself from the sign-in page.
- You have your email address and your password.

## Steps

### 1. Open the sign-in page

Open your Cascade space's address in your browser. If you are not signed in, you land on the **Connectez-vous à votre compte** (Sign in to your account) page. It is also displayed when you try to open a page of the application without being signed in: you will find that page again after signing in (step 4).

![The sign-in page, with the Email and Mot de passe (Password) fields](/aide/se-connecter/se-connecter-01-page-connexion.png)

### 2. Enter your credentials

Fill in **Email** and **Mot de passe** (Password), two required fields. Tick **Se souvenir de moi** (Remember me) if you want to stay signed in longer on this computer; don't do it on a shared computer.

![The Email and Mot de passe (Password) fields filled in, and the Se souvenir de moi (Remember me) box ticked](/aide/se-connecter/se-connecter-02-identifiants.png)

### 3. Click Se connecter

Click **Se connecter** (Sign in). If the address or the password is wrong, the message **Ces identifiants ne correspondent pas à nos enregistrements.** (These credentials do not match our records.) appears under the Email field, and a notification asks you to fix the form errors. The message is the same whatever the error: it does not say whether the address or the password is wrong. Fix it, then click **Se connecter** again.

![The error message under the Email field after incorrect credentials](/aide/se-connecter/se-connecter-03-erreur.png)

### 4. Get into the application

When the credentials are correct, you land on the page you wanted to open before being redirected to the sign-in, or otherwise on the **Tableau de bord** (Dashboard). If your account is not linked to any Cascade, you are taken to the creation of a Cascade: see [Create a Cascade](/en/aide/cascade-et-organisation/creer-une-cascade).

![The dashboard after signing in](/aide/se-connecter/se-connecter-04-tableau-de-bord.png)

### 5. Sign out when you are done

At the bottom of the menu, click your name, then **Déconnexion** (Sign out). You go back to the sign-in page. Remember to sign out on a computer that is not yours.

![Your account menu, with the Déconnexion (Sign out) entry](/aide/se-connecter/se-connecter-05-deconnexion.png)

## What happens next?

- You land in the space of your first Cascade. If your account has several, you switch Cascade from the menu at the top left, and this choice is remembered until you sign out. To find out more: see [Change the current Cascade](/en/aide/acces-et-compte/changer-de-cascade-courante).
- You stay signed in as long as you use the application. After a certain time without activity, you are sent back to the sign-in page. With **Se souvenir de moi** (Remember me), you stay signed in much longer, even after closing the browser.
- To change your password once you are signed in: see [Edit your account (details, password, theme)](/en/aide/acces-et-compte/modifier-son-compte).

## Good to know

- After 5 failed attempts with the same address from the same computer, sign-in is blocked for a short while. The message **Tentatives de connexion trop nombreuses. Veuillez essayer de nouveau dans … secondes.** (Too many sign-in attempts. Please try again in … seconds.) shows the waiting time. A successful sign-in resets the counter.
- There is no "Forgot password" link for now: if you can no longer find your password, contact the Cascade team.
- The **Retour au site public** (Back to the public site) button, at the top right, opens Cascade's presentation site in a new tab.
- The contributor and the signer do not have an account: they never use this page, they act from the links received by email.

## Frequently asked questions

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="I can't sign in even though my password is correct."}
Check the email address you entered: it is your account's, not necessarily the one of another tool. If you have made several attempts, wait for the indicated delay to end before trying again.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="I'm already signed in but I see the sign-in page."}
After a long inactivity, your session expired: sign in again, you will find the page you had opened.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="How do I stay signed in?"}
Tick **Se souvenir de moi** (Remember me) when you sign in, on your own computer only.
:::
::
