## Before you start

- You don't need a Cascade account: you act only from the link received by email. You receive this email because your name and email are listed as a signer in the subscription model. See [Create a subscription model](/en/aide/modeles-de-souscription/creer-un-modele-de-souscription).
- The contributor must have signed the contract before you. See [Sign a contract (contributor)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/signer-un-contrat).
- If the model requires supporting documents, they must have been uploaded by the contributor and then approved by the manager before you can sign. See [Approve or reject supporting documents](/en/aide/documents-justificatifs/valider-ou-rejeter-des-documents-justificatifs).

## Steps

### 1. Open the link received by email

As soon as the contract is ready to be countersigned, you receive an email with a link. Click it: the **Signature du contrat** (Contract signature) page opens (**Signature de l'avenant** (Amendment signature) if it is an amendment), with no username or password.

If you are also a Cascade user with the same email as the one listed in the model, you can also click **Signer** (Sign) at the end of the row in the **Souscriptions** (Subscriptions) list: the same page opens in a new tab.

### 2. Review the contract

The page displays the contract number, the organization in whose name it is drawn up (represented by the model's first signer), then the full text. If the model has attachments, they are listed in **Annexes envoyées** (Attachments sent): click a name to open it. When the contributor has uploaded supporting documents, they appear in **Justificatifs** (Supporting documents), each with its status.

You can also click **Télécharger** (Download) to get the contract as a PDF.

### 3. Certify that you have read it

Turn on the **Je certifie avoir lu le contrat** (I certify that I have read the contract) toggle (**Je certifie avoir lu l’avenant** (I certify that I have read the amendment) for an amendment). It is required.

### 4. Sign

Click **Signer** (Sign). The page displays **C'est signé !** (It's signed!) and **Votre signature a bien été prise en compte.** (Your signature has been recorded.) You can then download the signed contract with **Télécharger** (Download).

## What happens next?

- **Status**: the contract changes to **Signé** (Signed) in the subscription.
- **Emails**: the signed contract (PDF) is sent to the contributor, to the model's signers and to the organization's main manager.
- **Contributions**: for a model whose amount is set by the manager, the subscription's first contributions are created right away. The following ones are created automatically; see [Generate recurring contributions automatically](/en/aide/contributions/generer-automatiquement-les-contributions-recurrentes) and [What Cascade does every day](/en/aide/general/ce-que-cascade-fait-chaque-jour).
- **Contributor side**: they signed before you (see [Sign a contract (contributor)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/signer-un-contrat)) and receive the signed contract by email.

## Good to know

- Your link does not expire: you can reopen it later to download the contract. The contributor's, on the other hand, expires shortly after the subscription's start date.
- Before the contributor has signed (or while their supporting documents are being approved), the page only displays **En attente** (Pending): there is nothing to sign. You receive the email when it is your turn.
- The link is not personal: if the model has several signers, all of them receive the same email and the first one to sign signs for everyone. The other links then display **C'est signé !** (It's signed!).
- You don't type your name and you don't draw a signature: turning on **Je certifie avoir lu…** (I certify that I have read…) and then clicking **Signer** (Sign) counts as a signature. All the documents are in the name of the model's first signer.
- This page does not let you refuse the contract or cancel your signature: if the contract seems wrong to you, don't sign and tell the manager.
- For an amendment, the page and the steps are the same; only the titles and the certification sentence change. See [Make an amendment](/en/aide/souscriptions/faire-un-avenant).
- If the contract was added "without a signature", there is nothing to countersign. See [Add a contract without a signature (already signed on paper)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/ajouter-un-contrat-sans-signature).

## Frequently asked questions

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="I did not receive the email."}
Check your spam folder, then check with the manager that your address is indeed listed among the model's signers. The contract only reaches you when the contributor has signed (and, if there are any, their supporting documents have been approved).
:::
:::accordion-item{label="The page tells me “En attente” (Pending)."}
The contributor has not signed yet, or their supporting documents are still being approved. There is nothing for you to do: you will receive an email.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="The link already displays “C'est signé !” (It's signed!) although I haven't signed."}
Another signer of the model has probably signed before you with the same link: the contract is valid and you can download it.
:::
::
