## Before you start

- You don't need a Cascade account: you act only from the link received by email. It arrives when the manager sends you the contract (or amendment) for signature: see [Send a contract for signature](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/envoyer-un-contrat-en-signature). If you lost the email, ask the manager to resend the invitation: see [Resend the invitation to sign](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/relancer-l-invitation-a-signer).
- Your link is valid until 10 days after the subscription's effective date. After that, the page refuses to open: you then need to ask the manager for a new link.
- If the subscription model requires supporting documents, prepare them as PDF, JPEG or PNG. See [Define the supporting documents required](/en/aide/modeles-de-souscription/definir-les-documents-justificatifs-demandes).

## Steps

### 1. Open the link received by email

Click the link in the invitation email. A page opens, with no sign-in, with the Cascade logo. Its tab title is **Signature du contrat** (Contract signature), or **Signature de l'avenant** (Amendment signature) when the subscription has already been signed and is being modified (see [Make an amendment](/en/aide/souscriptions/faire-un-avenant)).

Depending on the state of the contract, the page shows you step 2 or goes straight to step 3.

### 2. Complete your information (if asked)

If the manager left some information for you to fill in, the page starts with **Complétez vos informations** (Complete your information): you cannot read the contract until you have validated it. It is organized in two blocks:

- **Facturation** (Billing): **Raison sociale ou nom** (Company name or name) (always offered, never required: it is a chance to correct the name recorded by the manager), then, only if the subscription model's sending method is **Facture** (Invoice), **Adresse** (Address), **Code postal** (Postal code), **Ville** (City), **Pays** (Country), **SIRET** (the label adapts to the country) and **Numéro de TVA** (VAT number). The address is required; the SIRET is only required if you are a company, that is, as soon as you fill in a SIRET or a VAT number.
- **Informations complémentaires** (Additional information): the custom fields that the contract template entrusts to you. They are all required.

The fields already filled in by the manager are displayed greyed out, and you cannot edit them. Click **Valider** (Validate): the page reloads with the contract, already completed with your information.

### 3. Read the contract

The full text of the contract (or amendment) is displayed. If it has attachments, an **Annexes envoyées** (Attachments sent) block lists them: click their name to open them in a new tab.

### 4. Upload the supporting documents (if the model asks for them)

A **Justificatifs à fournir** (Supporting documents to provide) block offers one field per document requested, all required. Upload each file in PDF, JPEG or PNG format. As long as some are missing, a message reminds you: « Envoyez d'abord tous les justificatifs requis ci-dessus pour pouvoir signer. » (First send all the required supporting documents above to be able to sign.) You sign and upload in one go, on the same page.

A **Justificatifs** (Supporting documents) block, at the bottom of the page, lists the documents already uploaded with their status (**En attente** (Pending), **Accepté** (Accepted) or **Rejeté** (Rejected)). A rejected document is displayed there with the reason for the refusal: you upload it again right here (see [Upload supporting documents](/en/aide/documents-justificatifs/deposer-des-documents-justificatifs)).

### 5. Sign

Turn on the **Je certifie avoir lu le contrat** (I certify that I have read the contract) switch (**Je certifie avoir lu l'avenant** (I certify that I have read the amendment) for an amendment), then click **Signer** (Sign). There is no signature to draw: the switch and the button count as a signature.

## What happens next?

The page reloads on an **En attente** (Pending) card: you have nothing more to do, and your signature cannot be cancelled from this page. What follows depends on the supporting documents:

- **With supporting documents**: the contract changes to **Documents à valider** (Documents to approve). The manager is notified by email, and so are you (your email tells you that your supporting documents are being approved). If they reject a document, you receive an email with a new link: the same form then only asks you for the missing or rejected documents, and you sign again. See [Approve or reject supporting documents](/en/aide/documents-justificatifs/valider-ou-rejeter-des-documents-justificatifs).
- **Without supporting documents, or once all the documents are accepted**: the contract changes to **À signer par le signataire** (To be signed by the signer). The signer(s) receive an email with their own link. Without supporting documents, you also receive an email telling you the contract is being signed. See [Countersign a contract (signer)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/contresigner-un-contrat).
- **Once the signer has signed**: the contract is **Signé** (Signed). You receive the signed contract as a PDF by email. If you reopen your link, the page displays **C'est signé !** (It's signed!), with no download button: keep the email.

The subscription's contributions are created by Cascade once the contract is signed on both sides (see [What Cascade does every day](/en/aide/general/ce-que-cascade-fait-chaque-jour)).

## Good to know

- A single link serves for your whole journey: completing your information, signing, then re-uploading a rejected supporting document. Each new email contains a link valid until the same deadline (10 days after the effective date).
- You cannot sign if a required piece of information or a supporting document is missing: the **Signer** (Sign) button stays blocked or the page points out the required document (« Ce justificatif est requis. » (This supporting document is required.)).
- You cannot edit the contract text. For any correction, contact the manager: they correct the subscription, as long as you have not signed, then send you the invitation again.
- Once you have signed, a change to the subscription goes through an amendment, which you will sign the same way.
- The manager can also record a contract already signed on paper, without going through this page: see [Add a contract without a signature (already signed on paper)](/en/aide/contrats-et-signature/ajouter-un-contrat-sans-signature).

## Frequently asked questions

::accordion
:::accordion-item{label="The link no longer opens."}
It has probably expired (10 days after the subscription's effective date) or was copied incorrectly. Ask the manager to send you a new invitation.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="I don't see the Sign button."}
Either you have already signed (the **En attente** (Pending) or **C'est signé !** (It's signed!) card is displayed), or you still have information to validate in step 2, or your supporting documents are being approved.
:::
:::accordion-item{label="Can I download the signed contract?"}
Not from the page. You receive it as a PDF by email once the signer has signed.
:::
::
