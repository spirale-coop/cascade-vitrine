::adaptive-contribution-steps
---
headline: How it works
title: "Four ingredients, one automatic calculation."
description: "Adaptive contribution isn't a case-by-case discount: it's an explicit rule, applied by Cascade to every member of the group."
steps:
  - title: A collective goal
    description: "The group sets the total amount to cover: shared expenses, group purchases, operating budget."
  - title: A distribution rule
    description: "Percentage of income, family quotient, tiered scale, or free contribution with a solidarity floor: each group chooses its own rule."
  - title: An automatic calculation
    description: "Cascade applies the rule to each member and recalculates their contribution as soon as a profile or the goal changes."
  - title: An adjustment at any time
    description: "The goal or the rule can change along the way; contributions readjust without any re-entry."
---
::

::adaptive-contribution-formulas
---
headline: Possible rules
title: "Each group chooses its own formula."
description: "Cascade doesn't impose a scale: it applies the one the group has decided on, and enforces it for every contribution."
formulas:
  - icon: i-lucide-percent
    title: Percentage of income
    description: "Everyone contributes in proportion to what they declare."
  - icon: i-lucide-users
    title: Family quotient
    description: "The amount takes household composition into account, as with a daycare center."
  - icon: i-lucide-layers
    title: Tiered scale
    description: "Tiers defined in advance, from the most supportive to the most comfortable."
  - icon: i-lucide-heart-handshake
    title: Free contribution with a solidarity floor
    description: "Everyone gives what they can, above a guaranteed minimum for the group."
