::problem
---
headline: Challenges
title: Standard tools aren't designed for collective organizations.
description: Time-consuming and hard to maintain, collective management is held back.
symptoms:
  - icon: i-lucide-files
    title: Management is time-consuming 
    description: Contracts, amendments, invoices, and more. Everything is scattered, hard to find, and hard to keep up to date.
  - icon: i-lucide-notebook-pen
    title: Tracking is manual
    description: Amounts and due dates are copied by hand across multiple tools.
  - icon: i-lucide-file-diff
    title: Resource pooling is poorly supported
    description: Cost allocation and shared rules are still calculated manually.
  - icon: i-lucide-receipt
    title: Processes are disconnected
    description: Contracts, contributions, and invoicing evolve separately, requiring duplicate data entry.
  - icon: i-lucide-users
    title: Transparency is difficult
    description: Sharing reliable, up-to-date information with the collective remains complex.
  - icon: i-lucide-table
    title: Models are hard to adapt
    description: Solidarity pricing and variable contributions don't fit well into standard tools.
