::economic-model
---
headline: Economic model
title: "A commission that follows usage, not software that captures value."
description: "Cascade draws on the model pioneered by CoopCycle, the federation of bike delivery cooperatives that showed a digital tool can be pooled and governed democratically instead of being captured by a private actor."
principles:
  - icon: i-lucide-percent
    title: Proportional to actual usage
    description: "Cascade takes a small share of the revenue managed through the platform — never a fixed subscription disconnected from a venue's actual activity."
  - icon: i-lucide-gauge
    title: Capped, never extractive
    description: "Beyond a certain threshold, the rate decreases: the goal is the federation's financial balance, not profit maximization."
  - icon: i-lucide-users
    title: Funded by its members
    description: "Cascade grows through the contributions of the organizations that use and govern it, in keeping with the Coopyleft license."
---

It's a way of staying true to what the project stands for — pooling resources rather than extracting value."
